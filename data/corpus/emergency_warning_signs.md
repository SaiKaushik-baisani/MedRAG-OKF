## When to call emergency services
Call your local emergency number immediately (112 in India) for chest pain or pressure, severe difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke, sudden severe headache, seizures, loss of consciousness, severe bleeding, suspected poisoning or overdose, or a serious allergic reaction.

## Signs of a heart attack
Heart attack symptoms include pressure, squeezing or pain in the centre of the chest, pain spreading to the arm, jaw, neck or back, shortness of breath, cold sweat, nausea and light-headedness. Do not drive yourself to hospital.

## Signs of a stroke
Remember FAST: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call emergency services. Sudden confusion, loss of vision, severe headache or loss of balance can also be signs.

## Signs of a severe allergic reaction
Swelling of the face, lips or throat, difficulty breathing or swallowing, wheezing, widespread hives, dizziness or collapse need emergency treatment.

## Mental health emergencies
If you or someone you know is thinking about suicide or self-harm, seek help immediately. In India, Tele-MANAS offers free, 24x7 support on 14416. Stay with the person and call emergency services if there is immediate danger.

## Fever warning signs
Seek urgent help for fever with stiff neck, confusion, a non-blanching rash, difficulty breathing, or fever in a baby younger than three months.
